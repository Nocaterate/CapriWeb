Just ask. She'll zap it.

Capri is an agent: her AI decides which of her powers to use, so you just talk normally. Press Ctrl Alt Space and speak, or Ctrl Alt T to type.

Anything that changes something asks you first with a Yes, do it / No / Always allow bubble. No passwords, no card fields, ever.

// 06 · setup

Up and zapping in 5 minutes

  1. 01

    Download

    Grab Capri-win64.zip from Releases, extract it, and double-click Capri.exe. She arrives with a lightning strike.

  2. 02

    Give her a brain

    Make a free key at console.groq.com, then right-click Capri → Settings… and paste it in.

  3. 03

    Talk to her

    Press Ctrl Alt Space and say "Open YouTube and search for lofi music".
